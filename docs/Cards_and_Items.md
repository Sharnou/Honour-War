## Automatic loot rules

- Items are automatically added to the hero inventory.
- Cards are automatically collected into the card collection.
- Materials are automatically collected into the material inventory.
- Equipment is automatically collected when its rarity passes the configured minimum.
- Pet Skill Items and Pet Refine Items can drop as bonus loot.
- MVPs provide guaranteed boss-card processing plus a chance for multiple premium rewards.
- MVP Bounty Tokens are an additional boss currency.
- `min_rarity` can be raised from Common to Uncommon/Rare/Epic/Legendary/MVP.
- `auto_pick_cards`, `auto_pick_items`, `auto_pick_materials`, `auto_pick_equipment`, and `pet_picks_up` are persisted loot-rule switches.
- No manual ground-item interaction is required by the current prototype.
